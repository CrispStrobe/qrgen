# AI QR Art - Feature Guide

## What is AI QR Art?

AI QR Art transforms boring QR codes into stunning visual designs while maintaining 100% scannability.

## How It Works

1. **High Error Correction**: Uses H-level (30%) error correction, allowing up to 30% of the QR code to be modified artistically while staying scannable

2. **Custom Module Shapes**: Instead of boring squares, uses artistic shapes:
   - Rounded corners
   - Circular dots
   - Organic liquid shapes
   - Geometric diamonds
   - Neon glow effects
   - Minimalist dots
   - Graffiti-style randomization
   - Watercolor transparency

3. **Brand Integration**: Logos blend naturally into the artistic design

4. **Multiple Variants**: Generates 3 unique variants each time

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Use High Error Correction**: Essential for artistic QR codes
2. **Test Scannability**: Always test with multiple QR scanner apps
3. **Keep Contrast**: Ensure good contrast between foreground and background
4. **Mind the Logo**: Keep logos 20-30% of QR code size
5. **Test Print Size**: Larger prints work better for complex designs
