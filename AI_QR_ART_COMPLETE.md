## 🚀 How to Use

1. Go to https://crispstrobe.github.io/qrgen
2. Click "Art Mode" button (top right)
3. Enter your URL or text
4. Choose an artistic style (8 options)
5. Adjust size (200-600px)
6. Keep error correction at "High (30%)"
7. Optionally add a logo
8. Click "✨ Create Artistic QR"
9. Download, copy, or share!

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Scannability:
- Use **High (30%)** error correction
- Maintain high contrast between foreground/background
- Test with multiple QR scanner apps
- Larger sizes (400px+) work better for complex styles

### For Best Aesthetics:
- Match QR colors to your brand
- Add your logo for recognition
- Use "Watercolor" for soft designs
- Use "Neon Glow" for digital brands
- Use "Graffiti" for street/urban brands

### For Print:
- Generate at 600px size
- Test printed size before mass production
- Use "Rounded" or "Geometric" for best scan rates
